# Grilling

- Map decisions and prerequisites. Ask answerable questions in chat at the user's pace. Wait before resolving dependent choices.
- Find accessible facts yourself. Separate facts, assumptions, and decisions. Probe edge cases.
- Recommend with reasons. For yes/no decisions, make "yes" accept your recommendation. Keep open trade-offs open.
- Stop when scope is sufficiently resolved. List open questions. Discussion grants no implementation authority.
- Use [domain-modeling](../domain-modeling/SKILL.md) in the same interview for terms or ADRs. Use [project-knowledge](../project-knowledge/SKILL.md) for durable records.
